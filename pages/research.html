01 Efficient computation

AI Accelerators & Automated Design

Efficient AI hardware depends on how data is reused, moved, and represented.

We co-design accelerator datapaths, on-chip interconnects, memory organization, and mappings so that hardware can adapt to changing layer shapes, sparsity patterns, and numerical precision.

Our work connects data-centric modeling in MAESTRO and sparse computation in SIGMA with dataflow switching in FEATHER and quantization-aware acceleration in MicroScopiQ. Learning-based methods, including ConfuciuX and AIRCHITECT, help navigate the large space of hardware configurations and workload mappings.

  • Dataflow & reuse
  • Sparsity & quantization
  • Reconfigurable hardware
  • Learning-based design

02 Scalable infrastructure

Distributed AI & Datacenter Co-design

Scaling AI requires more than adding compute.

Parallelism, collective communication, memory capacity, and network topology must work together. We model and co-design these layers to understand where time and resources are spent in distributed training and inference.

ASTRA-sim provides a foundation for system exploration; Themis and TACOS examine how communication should use the available network. FRED studies fabrics for wafer-scale training, while our datacenter roadmap and Chakra execution traces connect workload behavior to end-to-end platform design.

  • Distributed training & inference
  • Collective communication
  • Network topology
  • System modeling

03 Systems for emerging intelligence

Efficient & Compositional AI Systems

Modern AI combines neural computation with reasoning, irregular control flow, and interactions among models and tools.

We characterize these workloads and co-design algorithms, runtimes, and architectures around their compute and memory demands.

Our research spans XR workload benchmarking, KV-cache compression in GEAR and ThinKV, and neurosymbolic cognition in CogSys. SLM-MUX, compositional AI characterization, and ThunderAgent extend this work to coordinated models and program-aware agentic inference.

  • Efficient model inference
  • KV-cache compression
  • Neurosymbolic reasoning
  • Agentic & embodied workloads

04 Security as a systems constraint

Secure & Private Computing

Protecting data changes the bottlenecks of a computing system.

We study how architecture and system software can reduce the computation and communication costs of secure execution, encrypted inference, and privacy-preserving applications.

Our work connects NoC side-channel mitigation with efficient homomorphic-encryption operators and the use of AI ASICs for cryptographic computation. SCALE and PRIVATAR extend this approach to confidential distributed machine learning and secure offloading for multi-user virtual reality.

  • Homomorphic encryption
  • Confidential computing
  • Secure offloading
  • Side-channel mitigation

05 Moving data across hardware

Interconnects, Memory & Chiplet Systems

The cost of moving data depends on both the system abstraction and the physical implementation.

We study on-chip networks, virtual-memory mechanisms, heterogeneous chiplets, and 3D integration to connect compute and memory efficiently.

This work spans deadlock resolution in SWAP, interposer topology design in Kite, and translation coherence in ecoTLB. Heterogeneous network traversal, compute-on-memory integration, and multi-chip communication extend the same questions across routers, memory systems, chips, and packages.

  • Networks-on-chip
  • Virtual memory
  • Chiplets & interposers
  • 3D integration
