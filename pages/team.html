Principal Investigator

Tushar Krishna

Associate Professor

Tushar Krishna is an Associate Professor in the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Georgia Tech, with a courtesy appointment in Computer Science. He also serves as an Associate Director for the Center for Research into Novel Computing Hierarchies.

His research spans computer architecture, interconnection networks, networks-on-chip, and AI/ML accelerator systems, with a focus on optimizing data movement in modern computing platforms.
