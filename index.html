Computer architecture
& AI systems

We co-design accelerators, interconnects, and system software to make AI computing more efficient, scalable, and secure. Our research connects hardware and software through a shared focus on the movement of data.

Led by Prof. Tushar Krishna
School of Electrical and Computer Engineering
