FortiSky: Enhancing Adversarial and Bit-Error Robustness for Efficient and Secure Autonomous Systems
    Zishen Wan, Karthik Swaminathan, Nandhini Chandramoorthy, Pin-Yu Chen, Tushar Krishna, Vijay Janapa Reddi, Arijit Raychowdhury DATE 2026
    Abstract Paper
    systems

    Abstract

    FortiSky is a robust learning framework for autonomous UAVs that jointly improves adversarial and random bit-error robustness while maintaining high on-device energy efficiency via very-low-voltage operation.
