SCALE-Sim EVA: Design Principles for an Extensible, Visualizable, and Adaptable Accelerator Simulation Framework
    Jingtian Dang, Ritik Raj, Tushar Krishna arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.12354
    Summary Paper

    Summary

    SCALE-Sim EVA represents accelerator workloads as tensor commands executed by configurable hardware components. It tracks data readiness, placement, and resource availability, and produces traces for inspecting execution, memory occupancy, and contention.
