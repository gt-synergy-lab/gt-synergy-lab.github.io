LLMET: Enabling Cross-Layer Evaluation of Emerging M3D Memories for Energy-Efficient LLM Serving
    Ming-Yen Lee, Hanchen Yang, Faaiq Waqar, Harsono Simka, Tushar Krishna, Muhammed Ahosan Ul Karim, Shimeng Yu arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.26491
    Summary Paper

    Summary

    LLMET models how emerging monolithic 3D memory changes the energy cost of LLM inference. Its cross-layer simulations examine larger on-chip caches, reduced off-chip transfers, and the resulting trade-offs across server and edge platforms.
