A 40-nm Programmable Heterogeneous SoC With 5.625/0.85 MB RRAM/SRAM for Accelerating Neuro-Symbolic AI Models
    Che-Kai Liu, Zishen Wan, Yi-Sheng Noh, Mohamed Ibrahim, Samuel Spetalnick, Tushar Krishna, Win-San Khwa, Ashwin S. Lele, Yu-Der Chih, Meng-Fan Chang, Arijit Raychowdhury IEEE JSSC 2026
    Abstract Paper

    Abstract

    Presents a programmable 40-nm heterogeneous SoC with integrated RRAM/SRAM neural-symbolic data paths and scheduler-informed power management to accelerate neuro-symbolic AI workloads.
