Privatar: Scalable Privacy-preserving Multi-user VR via Secure Offloading

    Abstract

    Privatar enables scalable multi-user VR by securely offloading avatar reconstruction from headsets to untrusted local-network devices with provable privacy protections and minimal overhead.
