Closing the Efficiency Gap: AI Datacenter Co-design Roadmap for Scalable Training of LLMs
    Jesmin Jahan Tithi, Hanjiang Wu, Joongun Park, Avishaii Abuhatzera, Fabrizio Petrini, Tushar Krishna ICS 2026
    Abstract Paper
    ai distributed systems llm

    Abstract

    Presents Calculon-MoE, a datacenter co-design roadmap tool that explores compute, memory (HBM), and AI datacenter network topology and software strategies for scalable LLM/MoE training.
