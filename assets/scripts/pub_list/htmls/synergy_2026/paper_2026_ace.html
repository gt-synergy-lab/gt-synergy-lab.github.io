The ACE Center for Evolvable Computing
    Josep Torrellas, Minlan Yu, José F Martínez, Radu Teodorescu, Zhiru Zhang, Zhengya Zhang, Tarek Abdelzaher, Mohammad Alian, Adam Belay, Rajesh Gupta, Tushar Krishna, Arvind Krishnamurthy, Christos Kozyrakis, Charith Mendis, Subhasish Mitra, Muhammad Shahbaz, Rachee Singh, Steven Swanson, Michael Taylor, Mohit Tiwari, Mengjia Yan IEEE Micro 2026
    Summary Paper

    Summary

    The ACE Center investigates more efficient distributed computing through advances in processing, memory and storage, communication, and coordination. This overview also covers the security, privacy, and correctness of these systems.
