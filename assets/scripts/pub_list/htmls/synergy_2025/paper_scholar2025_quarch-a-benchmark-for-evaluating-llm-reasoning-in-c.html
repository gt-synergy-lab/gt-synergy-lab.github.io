QuArch: A Benchmark for Evaluating LLM Reasoning in Computer Architecture
    Shvetank Prakash, Andrew Cheng, Arya Tschand, Mark Mazumder, Varun Gohil, Jeffrey Ma, Jason Yik, Zishen Wan, Jessica A. Quaye, Elisavet Alvanaki, Avinash Kumar, Chandrashis Mazumdar, Tuhin Khare, Alexander Ingare, Ikechukwu Uchendu, Radhika Ghosal, Abhishek Tyagi, Chenyu Wang, Andrea Mattia Garavagno, Sarah Gu, Alice Guo, Grace Hur, Luca Carloni, Tushar Krishna, Ankita Nayak, Amir Yazdanbakhsh, Vijay Janapa Reddi ICML 2026

    llm systems algorithms simulation

    Abstract

    QuArch introduces a benchmark of expert-validated QA pairs to evaluate LLM knowledge and higher-order reasoning specifically for computer architecture tasks.
