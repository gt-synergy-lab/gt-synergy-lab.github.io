MIST: A Co-Design Framework for Heterogeneous, Multi-Stage LLM Inference
    Abhimanyu Rajeshkumar Bambhaniya, Hanjiang Wu, Suvinay Subramanian, Sudarshan Srinivasan, Souvik Kundu, Amir Yazdanbakhsh, Midhilesh Elavazhagan, Madhu Kumar, Minlan Yu, Arijit Raychowdhury, Tushar Krishna SC 2026
    Summary Paper

    Summary

    MIST evaluates multi-stage LLM inference across heterogeneous processors and memory hierarchies. It combines measurements and analytical models to study retrieval, KV reuse, reasoning, prefill, and decoding, including the effects of batching and concurrent workloads. Earlier versions appeared as Understanding and Optimizing Multi-Stage AI Inference Pipelines.
