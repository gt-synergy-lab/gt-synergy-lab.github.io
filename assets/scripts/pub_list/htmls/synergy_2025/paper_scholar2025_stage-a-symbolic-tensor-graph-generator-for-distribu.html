Scalable Synthesis of Distributed LLM Workloads Through Symbolic Tensor Graphs
    Changhai Man, Joongun Park, Hanjiang Wu, Huan Xu, Srinivas Sridharan, Tushar Krishna ISCA 2026
    Summary Paper Code

    Summary

    STAGE generates execution graphs for distributed LLM and mixture-of-experts workloads without requiring access to the target cluster. Symbolic tensor graphs support exploration of parallelization and hardware choices while tracking computation, memory, and communication at large scale.
