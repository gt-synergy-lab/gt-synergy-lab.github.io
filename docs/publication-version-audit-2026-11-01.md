# Publication version audit — November 1, 2026

## Scope and result

All 267 source fragments (248 visible archive records before this pass) were screened against the refreshed faculty publication list, the original lab list, and the arXiv author feed (113 records). Original arXiv versions and official proceedings/programs were consulted for title changes and conflicting dates. This is a version/year audit, not a verification of every author, abstract, award, or unrelated Scholar artifact.

The rendered archive now contains **228 records**: 19 redundant preprint entries were reconciled with 18 already-listed published works, and one duplicate SCALE-Sim preprint was collapsed. Raw fragments are retained for their aliases and supplemental links. Four other entries now explicitly identify a workshop, poster, or keynote instead of being mislabeled as preprints.

All 228 rendered records were checked for year-group/citation/badge agreement. No mismatches or duplicate title/citation pairs remain. There are 29 retained preprints; the consulted evidence did not establish a safe publication upgrade for them. This does not certify that none has been published elsewhere.

## Other corrections and cases kept separate

- The IMS sparse-FIR paper uses **2022**, correcting the faculty list’s 2021 typo. The [official IMS 2022 program](https://ims-ieee.org/sites/ims2019/files/content_images/IMS2022_Program_Book_Web.pdf) lists Th3E-5; DOI: `10.1109/IMS37962.2022.9865486`. This correction survives refreshes.
- SmartPAF now matches the faculty citation despite the omitted project-name prefix; it is not a new paper.
- “Rethinking Network Fabric Architectures for Distributed AI/ML Platforms” is **NoCArc 2024 (keynote)**, per the [organizer](https://www.nocarc.org/keynote-i) and [2024 contents](https://www.proceedings.com/content/077/077368webtoc.pdf).
- Chakra 2023 (`2305.14516`) and MLCommons Chakra 2026 (`2605.11333`) are distinct submissions with substantially different author lists. The [project citation](https://github.com/mlcommons/chakra) identifies the latter as MLSys 2026. They remain separate; neither receives an Oral label.
- VESPA is not replaced with SEESAW: the [SEESAW paper](https://synergy.ece.gatech.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/332/2018/04/seesaw_isca2018.pdf) compares against VESPA. A shared topic is insufficient for merging.
- PAF-FHE and SmartPAF remain separate pending an explicit version relationship. The [author page](https://jianmingtong.github.io/) separately lists the earlier PAF-FHE report. Similar methods and authors alone do not establish an identical publication.
- SCALE-Sim 2018 remains separate from the 2020 scalability-methodology paper; only its equivalent 2018 entries were deduplicated.
- The 2018 MAESTRO SRC poster, 2019 MICRO paper, and 2020 IEEE Micro article remain separate. STONNE conference/journal metadata is venue-specific; preprint aliases cannot leak between editions.
- The CPAL 2025 pruning paper remains separate from the ICML 2022 sparsity-workshop report.
